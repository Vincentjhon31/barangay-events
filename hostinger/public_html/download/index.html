How to install

Android

  1. Tap Download for Android.
  2. Open the downloaded file from your notifications or the Files app.
  3. If your phone asks, allow installing apps from your browser or Files app, then tap Install.
  4. Already have it? Installing a new version keeps your account and settings.

Windows

  1. Click Download for Windows and open the installer.
  2. If Windows says it protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Follow the steps. A new version installs over the old one; there's no need to remove it first.

The app tells you when a new version is out, and shows what's new.
