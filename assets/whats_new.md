## 1.17.0
- Chat with others on any public or group event: tap the round chat button on the event's details.
- Send emoji, a quick thumbs up, and edit or delete your own messages.
- Get notified when someone writes in a chat you're part of, and see which events have new messages right on the event list.
- New calendars like Sports can appear as their own tabs next to Public, Group, and Personal.
- Attach pictures and documents to your events again. Pictures are made smaller automatically before uploading.
- Use your own photo as your profile picture, or pick from more pictures in each category.
- The Calendar page now shows the current Philippine time.
- Notifications now play the app's own sound.
- A "What's new" note like this one appears after each update.
